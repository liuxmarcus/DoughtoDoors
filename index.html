私たちの台所 · Our kitchen

Baked at home, made for yours

Dough to Doors started in a home kitchen in Kolkata with one oven and a soft spot for Asian-style bakes — the airy chiffons, jiggly cheesecakes and pillowy breads that are hard to find done right here.

Nothing sits on a shelf. Every bake starts only after your order slip comes in, so what reaches your door was in the oven hours before — not days.

Have a flavour in mind or a date to plan around? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll write you into the baking schedule.
